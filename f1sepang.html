Why this weekend is different

Race weekend is the worst possible time to be stuck in a queue

Sepang has not hosted a Grand Prix since 2017. When the paddock returns in October, tens of thousands of visitors will arrive through KLIA across the same few days, most of them on the same flights, most of them heading in the same direction.

Immigration halls back up. Taxi ranks stretch. Hotels near the circuit fill months ahead. And every hour spent sorting logistics is an hour of practice or qualifying you paid to watch and did not see.

You have one weekend. We handle everything that is not the racing.

Questions

Before you book

How early should I book?

As early as you can. Race weekend capacity is finite, hotels near the circuit fill quickly, and we allocate hosts in booking order. If your flights are confirmed, message us now and we will hold your slot.

Which terminal do you cover?

We cover arrivals at KLIA Terminal 1 and Terminal 2. Tell us your flight number and we will confirm the exact meeting point when we book you in.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight and adjust. Your host is scheduled against your actual landing time, not your booked one, and your driver is briefed on the change. If the delay is significant, message us and we will rework the plan.

Can you arrange my hotel as well?

Yes. We coordinate accommodation as part of the Race Weekend and Private packages, including properties near the circuit and in Kuala Lumpur city centre. Tell us your budget and preferred area and we will come back with options.

Do you sell tickets to the race?

No. We are an independent travel concierge and we are not affiliated with the circuit, the championship, or any event organiser. Please buy tickets through official channels. We handle your arrival, transport, accommodation, and the logistics around your stay.

Is this only for race weekend?

Race weekend is what we are built around this October, but we operate year round for business travellers, families, and anyone who would rather not spend their first hour in Malaysia in a queue. Message us with your dates.
